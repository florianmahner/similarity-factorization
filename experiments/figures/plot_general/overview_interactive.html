Objects, words, and neural patterns can be compared pairwise to form similarity matrices. SRF decomposes these into sparse, non-negative dimensions that reveal the hidden structure.

Works with incomplete data

Real-world similarity data is often sparse: not every pair is measured. SRF optimizes directly on observed entries, predicting missing similarities from the learned dimensions.

Cross-validation on held-out entries selects the optimal number of dimensions k*, preventing both underfitting and overfitting.
